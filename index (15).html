Life in Northampton County

Two Great Towns,
One Great Life

Windward properties are located in both Northampton Borough and Walnutport — two charming Northampton County communities offering small-town character, outdoor beauty, and easy access to everything the Lehigh Valley has to offer.

🛣️
Route 145
The main north-south artery connecting both Northampton Borough and Walnutport directly to the Lehigh Valley corridor — making commutes simple from either property.
📍 Through both towns
🚗
US Route 22
Major east-west freeway linking Allentown, Bethlehem, and Easton — putting the entire Lehigh Valley within easy reach for commuters.
⏱ ~15 min south
🏎️
I-78 & I-476
Quick access to I-78 toward NYC and I-476 (PA Turnpike) toward Philadelphia — ideal for regional commuters and weekend travelers.
⏱ ~20 min south
🏙️
Allentown & Bethlehem
Two of Pennsylvania's largest cities are only 20 miles south via Routes 145 and 22 — offering major employers, entertainment, and dining.
⏱ ~25 min drive

Northern Lehigh School District
Walnutport residents are served by Northern Lehigh School District, while Northampton Borough residents are served by the highly rated Northampton Area School District — both offering strong K–12 education.
📍 Serving both communities

Local K–6 elementary school serving over 670 students, conveniently located on Blue Mountain Drive right in the community.

Jack Frost Halloween Parade
Northampton Borough's beloved annual Halloween parade — known locally as the Jack Frost Parade — winds through Main Street every October. The parade typically takes place on the second to last Thursday of October at 7pm, starting at the corner of Smith and Laubach, continuing up Laubach to 19th Street, along 19th to Main, and down Main Street to Lerchenmiller Drive. A true community tradition right outside your door.
📅 October 2026 · Main St, Northampton

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Windward Properties ("we," "us," or "our") operates windward.live and the Windward Resident Portal. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or use our services. By using our site you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Contact Form Inquiries
When you submit our contact form we collect your name, email address, phone number, and message content. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Resident Portal Accounts
For tenants using the Resident Portal we collect your name, email address, phone number, unit information, lease details, and payment history. This information is necessary to provide property management services.

Payment Information
Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your card number, CVV, or full payment details on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule showings
  • Manage your tenancy and lease agreement
  • Process rent payments and maintain payment history
  • Send maintenance request updates
  • Send important notices about your tenancy
  • Comply with legal obligations as a landlord

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Our website uses the following third party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — Secure database and authentication. Your account credentials and tenant data are stored on Supabase's servers with industry-standard encryption.
  • Stripe — Payment processing. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe and are never stored on our servers. Stripe is PCI DSS compliant.
  • DocuSign — Electronic document signing. Lease agreements signed through DocuSign are stored securely on DocuSign's servers.
  • Google Maps — Location display on our website. Google's privacy policy governs data collected through map interactions.
  • Resend — Email delivery for contact form confirmations and notifications.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Row Level Security so tenants can only access their own data
  • Secure session management that clears on browser close
  • No third party advertising trackers or session recorders on our site
  • All API keys stored in secure server environments, never in public code

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising cookies or third party tracking cookies. Google Maps may set cookies when you interact with the map on our locations page.

7. Your Rights

As a resident or site visitor you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data after your tenancy ends
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights contact us at contact.windward.live@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify current tenants of any significant changes via email. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

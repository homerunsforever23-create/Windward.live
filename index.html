Life in Northampton County

Two Great Towns,
One Great Life

Windward properties are located in both Northampton Borough and Walnutport — two charming Northampton County communities offering small-town character, outdoor beauty, and easy access to everything the Lehigh Valley has to offer.

🛣️
Route 145
The main north-south artery connecting both Northampton Borough and Walnutport directly to the Lehigh Valley corridor — making commutes simple from either property.
📍 Through both towns
🚗
US Route 22
Major east-west freeway linking Allentown, Bethlehem, and Easton — putting the entire Lehigh Valley within easy reach for commuters.
⏱ ~15 min south
🏎️
I-78 & I-476
Quick access to I-78 toward NYC and I-476 (PA Turnpike) toward Philadelphia — ideal for regional commuters and weekend travelers.
⏱ ~20 min south
🏙️
Allentown & Bethlehem
Two of Pennsylvania's largest cities are only 20 miles south via Routes 145 and 22 — offering major employers, entertainment, and dining.
⏱ ~25 min drive
🎓
Northern Lehigh School District
Walnutport residents are served by Northern Lehigh School District, while Northampton Borough residents are served by the highly rated Northampton Area School District — both offering strong K–12 education.
📍 Serving both communities
🏫
Lehigh Elementary School
Local K–6 elementary school serving over 670 students, conveniently located on Blue Mountain Drive right in the community.
📍 800 Blue Mountain Dr
🎒
Northampton Area School District
Residents at our Northampton Borough properties (1796 Main St & 1775 Franklin St) are served directly by the well-regarded Northampton Area School District — known for strong academics and community involvement.
📍 Northampton Borough
🚶
Lehigh Canal Trail
A beloved walking and biking path along the historic Lehigh Canal — perfect for morning jogs, evening strolls, and weekend rides right in town.
📍 Along the canal
⚾
Local Parks & Athletic Fields
Both Northampton Borough and Walnutport have community parks and athletic fields — perfect for weekend recreation, youth sports, and family gatherings year-round.
📍 In both boroughs
⛷️
Blue Mountain Resort
Pennsylvania's highest ski area is right nearby — offering skiing, snowboarding, and summer outdoor adventures against stunning Blue Mountain views.
⏱ ~15 min drive
🛶
Lehigh River Recreation
The Lehigh River borders the borough and offers fishing, kayaking, tubing, and scenic walks — a true outdoor gem for nature lovers.
📍 Borough waterfront
🎬
Becky's Drive-In
A beloved local landmark in neighboring Lehigh Township — a classic drive-in diner and community staple loved by residents of all ages.
⏱ ~5 min drive
🍝
A Ca Mia's Italian Restaurant
A highly regarded upscale Italian restaurant nearby in Lehigh Township — perfect for date nights or special occasions just minutes from home.
⏱ ~5 min drive
🍔
Local & Chain Dining
Both Northampton Borough and Walnutport have a variety of local favorites and dining options nearby — with even more choices minutes away in neighboring Catasauqua, Slatington, and the greater Lehigh Valley.
📍 In & around both towns
🏥
St. Luke's Hospital – Walnutport
A St. Luke's University Health Network facility located right in Walnutport — convenient healthcare for residents at our 917 Main St property without leaving the community.
📍 330 N Best Ave, Walnutport
⚕️
St. Luke's – Palmerton Campus
Full-service hospital campus in nearby Palmerton, PA — part of the acclaimed St. Luke's University Health Network serving Carbon and Northampton counties.
⏱ ~15 min north
🩺
Lehigh Valley Hospital – Cedar Crest
One of Pennsylvania's top-rated health systems, Lehigh Valley Health Network's flagship Cedar Crest campus in Allentown is easily accessible via Route 145 and Route 22.
⏱ ~25 min south
🛒
Giant Food Market
A full-service Giant supermarket serving the Walnutport area, and additional grocery options conveniently accessible from our Northampton Borough properties — everyday essentials always close by.
📍 Near both communities
🏬
Shopping in Both Boroughs
Northampton Borough has a lively Main Street with local shops and services, while Walnutport's retail strip along Main Street puts everyday conveniences within walking distance of all three properties.
📍 Main Street, both towns
🎡
Lehigh Valley Mall
The region's premier shopping destination in Whitehall — featuring major retailers, dining, and entertainment. Easily accessible via Route 145 south to Route 22.
⏱ ~25 min south
